Pump Support Structure

Structural Design FEA Machining Testing

Designed, analyzed, and fabricated a structural support frame for a belt-driven centrifugal pump. Symbolic stress and deflection analysis, material selection, and FEA guided the design before physical testing.

Testing also highlighted the effects of pin-joint compliance and the differences between idealized analytical models, FEA, and real-world structural behavior.

MACHINE LEARNING · GEOSPATIAL DATA

Wildfire Burn Severity Predictor

I applied machine learning techniques using a Random Forest classifier in Python to predict wildfire burn severity based on Landsat 8 imagery, SRTM elevation data, and existing burn-severity data.

The model achieved approximately 70% classification accuracy, demonstrating the potential for data-driven wildfire management strategies.

X-BOW SYSTEMS

Manufacturing Engineering Intern

Summer 2026

At X-Bow Systems, I worked on manufacturing engineering projects focused on improving production efficiency as the company scales.

I developed a manufacturing execution system to track production and building occupancy with an integrated andon light system, and automated material requests using Python and Excel VBA, reducing a roughly eight-minute process to about 30 seconds. I also built a new work center that gave a 37% time reduction per assembly.

Across several projects, I implemented improvements projected to save approximately 368 labor hours annually — equivalent to 46 eight-hour working days — while improving traceability, reducing human error, and lowering risks of production stoppages.
